6. Live Tracking and location sharing

Live Tracking is off by default and only works when you turn it on and register a Share Code.

What gets shared. While Live Tracking is on and a run is active, your device sends your location and run data to our servers. Depending on what you’ve chosen, it can be seen in the in-app Track Runners view, the watch app and the web leaderboard, or obtained through the network services, by:

  • anyone who has your Share Code (including through a link or QR code you share);
  • everyone following a group run or race you join; and
  • for public races, anyone who finds the race in the calendar or has its link.

Your position, distance, pace, heading, predictions, wave, route, and finished, stopped or off-route status may be shown. Finished or stopped runners’ positions are hidden, positions are only served for a limited time after your phone recorded them, and runners drop off leaderboards a while after their last update. The current time limits are described in the [Privacy Policy](https://livepace.fit/privacy-policy/) and may change as we improve the Services.

Background location. Live Tracking uses your location in the background while a run is active. You can stop it at any time by ending the run, turning off Live Tracking, or revoking location permission in your device settings.

Your responsibilities. By turning on Live Tracking you agree that:

  • you are choosing to make your real-time location visible to others, which can reveal where you are, where you started or finished, and your routines;
  • once someone has your Share Code or a race link, we cannot control who they pass it on to;
  • you should think carefully about sharing your location publicly, especially around your home, workplace or regular routes; and
  • you must not use Live Tracking to share another person’s location without their knowledge and consent.

No guarantee of delivery. Positions may be delayed, rejected, out of date or missing because of GPS, network, device, battery or server conditions. A position that has not reached our servers may be shown as not delivered, and the app may show “Stalled” when nothing has got through. See Section 3; Live Tracking is not a safety service.

Run logs. The app may keep raw logs of finished runs on your device for a configurable number of days to help with troubleshooting. You control this in the app’s settings.

Summary: Live Tracking is opt-in. When it’s on, people with your code or race link can see where you are. Don’t share it — or anyone else’s location — carelessly.
