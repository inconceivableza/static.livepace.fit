Privacy Policy

Supplied by Inconceivable R and D (Pty) Ltd

Effective Date: 21 November 2026  |  Last Updated: 21 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to LivePace. This Privacy Policy explains how Inconceivable R and D (Pty) Ltd (“we”, “us”, or “our”), a company registered in South Africa, handles information in connection with the LivePace mobile application (the “App”), the LivePace watch app for Garmin watches (the “Watch App”) and the LivePace leaderboard web application (the “Web App”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

2. About LivePace

LivePace is a pace-tracking application that allows you to:

  • Plan running races and record both races and training runs
  • Set targets, expected paces, and checkpoints
  • Estimate predicted arrival times at each checkpoint and the finish line
  • Optionally share live updates and predictions with supporters

The App is designed with privacy as a core principle. Since sharing information is a core feature, that sharing is done with encryption and limited with access control.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Enter Into the App

All data you enter into LivePace — including race names, checkpoint names, distances, paces, and timing information — is stored on your device. Unless you choose to share, this data:

  • Is never transmitted to our servers or any server operated by or on behalf of Inconceivable R and D
  • Is never shared with third parties by us
  • Remains entirely under your control
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear the App’s data

If you do choose to share, information about runs including race names, distances, paces, and timing information - both planned and actual - and live location data and predictions, will be:

  • Transmitted to our servers or servers operated by or on behalf of Inconceivable R and D, over a secure encrypted channel
  • Transmitted from those servers to recipients having the necessary Share Code to access your data
  • Stored on those servers for recipients to query
  • Deleted from those servers if you request your Share Code to be deleted

Since the Share Code allows anyone with it to obtain your information, you should take care not to allow it to be known except by those you trust to access that information.

3.2 No User Accounts

The current version of LivePace does not require or support the creation of user accounts. We do not collect your email address, or any other identifying information, except for an optional Name that is associated with your Share Code.

3.3 Automatic Data Collection

LivePace does not use analytics libraries, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting tools that transmit data off your device. We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, usage statistics, or diagnostic information.

Note: The underlying mobile operating system (Android or iOS) and the app store through which you downloaded LivePace (Google Play or Apple App Store, or for the Watch App, Garmin’s Connect IQ Store) may independently collect certain data such as download statistics or crash reports, subject to their own privacy policies. These collections are outside our control.

3.4 The Watch App for Garmin Watches

The Watch App is an optional data field for Garmin watches, installed from Garmin’s Connect IQ Store. It works only together with the App on the phone paired with your watch. It sends nothing to our servers, or to anyone else, by itself.

While the Watch App is in use in an activity on your watch, it sends the following to the App on your paired phone, and to nothing else:

  • The activity’s state: when it is started, paused, resumed and stopped
  • Activity data recorded by the watch: distance, elapsed and timer time, speed, heart rate, cadence, altitude, ascent and descent, energy expenditure, calories, and the activity’s sport and name
  • The watch’s GPS positions, with their accuracy, altitude, heading and speed
  • Information about the watch and the Watch App: the watch’s model, its battery level and memory use, and the state of the Watch App’s connection to the phone

The App sends the Watch App what it shows: the state of your run, when your position was last shared, the supporter points on your route, and the names, positions and distances of the runners and supporters you follow.

The watch and the phone exchange these messages over Bluetooth. On Android they pass through Garmin’s Garmin Connect app on your phone; on iPhone they go directly between the watch and the App. Garmin’s handling of data in its own apps and services is governed by Garmin’s own privacy policy.

The App treats what the Watch App sends as part of your run, as described in section 3.1: it is stored on your device with the run, and remains under your control. The watch’s positions and distance are used in tracking your run, so if you share your run through Live Tracking (section 4.1), the live location that is shared may come from your watch. Heart rate, cadence, energy expenditure, calories and the information about the watch are not sent to our servers.

3.5 The Leaderboard Web Application

The Web App is a web page that shows the live leaderboards of races and group runs. We serve it at live.livepace.fit, and it also opens at track.livepace.fit when you open a link shared from the App on a device where the App is not installed to open it. It shows a calendar of public races and, for the race or group run you choose, a map and a table of the runners in it. Anyone can use it: it has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not ask for your location or use any sensor of your device.

Anyone who opens a race’s or group run’s leaderboard sees, for each runner on it:

  • The name associated with their Share Code, but not the Share Code itself. The page’s code carries a unique identifier for each runner, by which the same runner can be recognised across races
  • The route they are on, and whether they are racing or training
  • Whether they have not started, are running, have finished or have stopped
  • Their start time, time elapsed, distance covered and pace, and their expected or actual finish time
  • When their phone last sent an update, and whether they have left their route
  • While their run is under way, their last position and heading, shown on the map

The Web App works out predicted arrival times at checkpoints from these figures, in the visitor’s browser. A runner’s position is not shown once they have finished or stopped, or once it is more than 4 hours old, and a runner is removed from the leaderboard 6 hours after their phone last sent an update. Deleting your Share Code (section 8) removes you from every leaderboard at once.

A public race’s leaderboard can be opened by anyone, and public races are listed in the Web App’s race calendar. A group run is not listed: its leaderboard can be opened by anyone who has its link. A group run’s page also shows what its creator gave: its name, date and start time, any start, finish and support points placed on its map, and its routes, which anyone who opens it can download as the GPX files the creator added. Opening a runner’s share link while that runner is in a race shows their name, says that they are running now, and leads to that race’s leaderboard, where every runner in it is shown, not only the runner the link is for.

To show its pages, your browser asks our servers for the list of public races and for the race or group run you open, its routes and its leaderboard, and asks for the leaderboard again every 10 seconds while it is open. If you open a link that carries a runner’s Share Code (a share link, or a leaderboard link naming runners to follow), your browser sends that Share Code to our servers to find the runner and any race they are in now, and with each request for the leaderboard, to pick out those runners. Your browser also asks our servers for a location hint, used only to pre-select a country in the race calendar: our servers do not look up your location from your IP address; they return only a country, region or city that a network service in front of them has added to your request, if any, and they do not keep it.

The Web App sets no cookies. Your browser’s local storage keeps only your display settings and the location the race calendar is filtered by, which is sent with requests for the calendar; clearing this site’s data in your browser removes them. A Share Code in the address of a page you open stays in your browser’s history.

Your browser fetches the map images from the OpenStreetMap Foundation’s tile servers, which receive your IP address and the area of the map shown, and handle them under the Foundation’s own privacy policy. Everything else the Web App loads is served by us, and it includes no analytics, advertising or error-reporting service.

Our servers’ logs of these requests hold neither your IP address nor your browser, and any Share Code in an address they record is replaced by a one-way fingerprint. The logs stay on our servers for a limited period of time.

4. How You May Share Your Data

4.1 Voluntary Sharing via Live Updates

LivePace includes a Live Tracking feature that allows you to share your race progress live with supporters. When you enable this feature by registering a share code, and turning on Live tracking enabled:

  • Your selected race information, including name and distance and route, will be shared with our servers and servers operated by or on behalf of Inconceivable R and D
  • Your live location will be tracked (including when the app is in the background), and shared with our servers and servers operated by or on behalf of Inconceivable R and D
  • The race information and live location, as well as predictions of your future race progress, will be accessible from those servers by anyone with your Share Code.
  • If you join or create a race or group run, your live position, distance, pace and heading are additionally published on that event’s public leaderboard. That leaderboard is visible to anyone who has the race or group-run link (for example, a helper watching from a support table’s web browser) - a wider audience than the one supporter you share a Share Code with. Public runs are also linked from the main page, so are searchable by anyone needing to know a link. This too is voluntary: it happens only for a race or group run you have chosen to join or create. What a leaderboard shows, and who can open it, is described in section 3.5.
  • This sharing is entirely voluntary and initiated only by you, and will grant permission to any request possessing your Share Code to retrieve and/or be updated live with the shared information

We do retain and process the content of this shared information on our servers and servers operated by or on behalf of Inconceivable R and D. Please note that this feature:

  • Is entirely optional — the App will remain fully functional without it
  • Requires your explicit permission to access your device’s location, which you may grant or revoke at any time through your device settings
  • Access locations data only while the feature is actively enabled during a run; it will access your location in the background if Live Tracking is enabled and a run is active, but will not otherwise access your location in the background when you are not using the App and a run is not active.
  • Store location data locally on your device in the first instance
  • Transmits your location data to our servers and shares it with supporters via the Share Code mechanism

Location data is among the most sensitive categories of personal information. We are committed to using it only for the direct purpose of tracking your run and improving your in-app experience, and never for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties. Any future changes to this feature will be accompanied by clear in-app disclosures and will comply with all applicable platform requirements (including Google Play and Apple App Store location permission policies) and with POPIA and any other applicable data protection laws.

4.2 Voluntary Sharing via Text Message

LivePace includes an optional feature that allows you to share your race progress with supporters. When you use this feature:

  • The App generates a formatted text message containing your race progress and plan details (checkpoints, paces, and estimated arrival times)
  • You are then prompted to share this text using your device’s standard system share facility
  • You choose which app or contact to share with (e.g., WhatsApp, SMS, email)
  • This sharing is entirely voluntary and initiated only by you

We do not have access to, retain, or process the content of messages you share, or to whom you share them. The sharing occurs entirely on your device through your chosen messaging application.

4.3 Your Responsibility When Sharing

When you choose to share your race data via text message, please be aware that the recipient and any third-party messaging service you use to transmit that data will handle it according to their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage you to be mindful of the information you share and with whom.

When you choose to share your race data via Live Tracking, please be aware that the Share Code will itself grant access to that data, so the recipient and any person or device or system that gains that Share Code will be able to access the data.

5.1 Optional Cloud Synchronisation

A future version of the App may offer an optional account-based cloud synchronisation service, which would allow your race data to be stored on our servers and accessed from multiple devices. This feature would:

  • Be entirely optional — the App will remain fully functional without it
  • Require you to create an account and affirmatively opt in
  • Involve your race data being transmitted to and stored on our servers
  • Be governed by an updated privacy policy published at the time of the feature’s release, which will detail the data collected, how it is stored and secured, and your rights regarding that data

Note that this will extend beyond, and possibly replace, the Share Code-based Live Tracking sharing described above

6. Data Storage and Security

Your race and run data in LivePace is:

  • Stored within the App’s designated storage area on your device, using the standard secure storage mechanisms provided by your operating system. The security of your locally stored data is governed by your device’s own security features, including any screen lock or encryption you have enabled.
  • Transmitted only over encrypted channels to our servers or servers operated by or behalf of Inconceivable R and D.
  • Stored on those servers in order to provide that data to supporters, solely for the purposes described above, and not made available to any third party without the Share Code provided to you, except what the leaderboard of a race or group run you join shows to anyone who opens it (sections 4.1 and 3.5).
  • Transmitted only over encrypted channels in response to requests for that data with the Share Code required to access it, or, for a leaderboard, to anyone who opens it.

We cannot be held responsible for the security of data on your personal device. We recommend keeping your device’s operating system and the App updated to benefit from the latest security patches.

LivePace is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in jurisdictions where a higher age threshold applies, including South Africa in respect of data protection principles applicable to minors). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not permit children to use the Live Tracking feature of the App or create Share Codes; other usage should be under appropriate parental supervision.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Because we do not collect, process, or store any personal information about you in the current version of LivePace, many standard data subject rights (such as access, rectification, erasure, and portability) are exercised entirely by you, on your own device. Specifically:

You have full control over all the data stored by the App on your own device:

  • You can view all data stored by the App within the App itself
  • You can delete any or all of your data within the App at any time
  • You can delete all of the App’s data by clearing the App’s storage via your device settings
  • You can remove all data entirely by uninstalling the App

Since personal information is transmitted to and stored on our servers or servers operated by or on behalf of Inconceivable R and D, you also have rights over that data:

  • We will not share that data with any third party except via your Share Code which you are in control of, and on the leaderboard of a race or group run you join (section 3.5).
  • You can request all data stored on our servers using your Share Code.
  • You can request all data associated with your Share Code to be deleted from our servers. This can be done within the app, by following these steps:
    • Go to Settings -> Live Tracking
    • Beneath your Share Code, choose Delete Your Share
    • When prompted to Delete share and all your data, read the explanation and then choose Confirm if you would like to delete it.

Residents of South Africa have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA). Residents of other jurisdictions may have rights under other applicable laws (such as the GDPR for European residents).
