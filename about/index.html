LivePace Running App

LivePace is for runners who want to track their pace and expected progress, and share that with supporters during races or other runs. It supports Android and iOS phones, and also a Garmin watch data field that sends and receives updates and keeps the runner informed without having to look at their phone.

It can be used for races along a route, using the course distance covered for predictions even though runners actually travel slightly further. A web leaderboard supports tracking runners for public races or non-public group runs (in both cases if the runner shares that information).

For more information, see the Features page or try the app out yourself
